# Pets

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundation
**Category:** Experience

---

# Purpose

This document describes the Pet experience inside Cosmos.

Pets exist to make the Base feel alive.

They are companions to the environment rather than assistants for productivity.

---

# Overview

Version 1 provides one Pet.

The Pet permanently lives inside the Base.

Unlike the Companion, Pets do not perform tasks or assist with work.

Their purpose is purely atmospheric.

---

# Object Model

The Pet is an Object with permanent identity. Theme Components define its appearance while its role and Base membership remain unchanged.

---

# Role

Pets exist to make the Base feel inhabited.

They create warmth, personality and life without distracting from productive work.

They are companions to the user's home rather than the user's workflow.

---

# Behavior

Version 1 intentionally keeps Pet behavior simple.

Pets may:

- sit
- sleep
- perform subtle idle animations
- occasionally change position

Their movements should remain slow, calm and natural.

Pets should never become distracting.

---

# Presence

Pets live exclusively inside the Base.

They never appear:

- inside the Cosmos
- inside Workspaces
- inside tool windows

The Base is their home.

---

# Interaction

Version 1 allows only minimal interaction.

Examples include:

- selecting the Pet
- petting the Pet

Future versions may expand these interactions.

The Pet should always remain optional.

---

# Theme Support

Themes may completely replace the Pet's appearance.

Examples include:

- robot
- cat
- fox
- bird
- fantasy creature

Themes may replace:

- appearance
- animations
- sounds
- visual effects

They must never change the Pet's purpose.

---

# Living Environment

The Pet belongs to the Base.

Closing the Base does not reset the Pet.

The Pet simply continues existing inside its own environment.

Future versions may introduce richer routines while preserving the calm atmosphere.

---

# Future

Future versions may introduce:

- multiple Pets
- custom Pets
- advanced idle routines
- decorative interactions
- environment interactions
- collectible Pets

These additions should enrich the feeling of a living home without affecting productivity.

---

# Experience Goals

Pets should always feel:

- cozy
- peaceful
- charming
- alive
- unobtrusive

Users should see Pets as part of their home rather than as interactive features.

---

# Scope

This document describes the Pet experience.

Future AI, automation or gameplay systems are intentionally outside the scope of Pets.
