# Main Room

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundation
**Category:** Experience

---

# Purpose

This document describes the Main Room of the Base.

The Main Room is the first place users enter after opening the Base.

It introduces the two primary workspaces of Cosmos while providing a calm and familiar environment for planning, research and creation.

---

# Overview

The Main Room serves as the central hub of the Base.

Unlike later Rooms, it is permanently available and contains the user's most frequently used workspaces.

Version 1 provides two fixed workspaces:

- Knowledge Workspace
- Creation Workspace

Together they form the foundation of productive work inside Cosmos.

---

# Layout

The room should feel compact, comfortable and immediately understandable.

The user should recognize the entire room without needing to explore it.

The overall layout follows a simple composition:

- Knowledge Workspace on the left
- Creation Workspace on the right
- Companion seated in the center
- Pet near the Companion
- One visible doorway leading to additional Rooms

The room should remain visually balanced and uncluttered.

---

# Knowledge Workspace

The Knowledge Workspace represents research and understanding.

Within the Cosmos Theme it appears as a desk containing:

- notebook
- computer
- knowledge related objects

It provides access to reading, planning and knowledge-oriented work.

---

# Creation Workspace

The Creation Workspace represents creation and implementation.

Within the Cosmos Theme it appears as a workbench.

It provides access to creation-oriented tools and future creative workflows.

Future extensions may expand this workspace without changing its role.

---

# Companion

The Companion occupies the visual center of the room.

Version 1 keeps the Companion seated when the room is entered.

The Companion acts as the social center of the Base without demanding attention.

Future versions may expand the Companion's routines and interactions.

---

# Pet

The Pet remains close to the Companion.

Version 1 limits the Pet to simple idle behavior.

Its primary purpose is to make the environment feel inhabited.

---

# Room Navigation

Version 1 contains one visible doorway leading to the next Room.

Future versions may introduce additional Rooms, corridors or alternative navigation methods.

The Main Room should always remain the central starting point of the Base.

---

# Workspace Interaction

Workspace objects are clearly recognizable as interactive elements.

Hovering over them should display:

- subtle outline
- soft glow

Selecting a Workspace opens it as a fixed, full-size Workspace Environment Window.

Version 1 prioritizes maximum working space over maintaining visibility of the room.

---

# Atmosphere

The Main Room should feel:

- welcoming
- calm
- organized
- personal
- productive

It should resemble the workshop of a researcher rather than the interface of an application.

The room should invite users to continue their work naturally.

---

# Theme Support

Every Theme may completely replace the appearance of the Main Room.

Examples include:

- spaceship bridge
- fantasy home
- tree house
- modern studio

The universal room structure remains identical.

Workspace positions, navigation and interaction must remain consistent across all Themes.

---

# Future

Future versions may introduce:

- Base Builder
- movable workspace objects
- custom decorations
- additional workspace objects
- ambient activities
- expanded Companion behavior
- multiple room layouts

These additions should build upon the existing room structure rather than replacing it.

---

# Experience Goals

The Main Room should always feel:

- familiar
- comfortable
- cozy
- purposeful
- expandable

Users should immediately understand that this is their personal place for thinking, creating and continuing their work.

---

# Scope

This document describes only the Main Room.

Additional Rooms, Workspaces, the Companion and Pets are documented separately.
