# Room

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Authoritative  
**Category:** Base

---

# Purpose

Rooms extend the functionality of the Base.

They provide dedicated environments for specific activities while preserving the feeling of walking through a living ship.

Every Room should feel like a real place rather than a software page.

---

# Overview

Rooms are physically connected to the Base through doors.

Each Room serves a unique purpose and may contain one or more Workspaces.

Rooms are architectural spaces rather than interface elements.

---

# Object Template

## Architecture

Every Room has its own architectural identity.

Rooms are not required to share the same layout.

Their structure should naturally support their intended purpose.

---

## Entrance

Every Room is entered through a visible doorway.

The entrance should always remain recognizable to preserve orientation inside the ship.

Users should immediately understand how to return to the previous Room.

---

## Interior

Rooms remain visually open and readable.

Furniture and decorative elements should support the Room's purpose without cluttering the environment.

---

## Workspaces

Rooms may contain any number of Workspaces.

Workspace placement should feel intentional and integrated into the surrounding architecture.

---

## Navigation

Movement between Rooms should feel natural.

Transitions should reinforce the illusion of physically walking through the ship.

---

# Visual Identity

Rooms are recognized by:

- unique architecture
- recognizable purpose
- integrated Workspaces
- visible entrance
- distinct atmosphere

Each Room should be memorable and immediately identifiable.

---

# Cosmos Theme

## Architecture

The active Theme defines:

- walls
- floors
- ceilings
- furniture
- lighting
- decorative elements

Every Room should visually communicate its intended purpose.

---

## Lighting

Lighting should reinforce the mood of the Room.

Examples include:

Library

- warm
- calm

Workshop

- bright
- practical

Laboratory

- clean
- focused

---

## Decoration

Decoration supports identity without overwhelming functionality.

Every object should feel like it belongs inside the Room.

---

# Visual States

Rooms support:

- default
- entered
- Workspace opened

Visual changes should remain subtle.

---

# Animations

Version 1 animations remain restrained.

Examples include:

- smooth Room transitions
- subtle ambient lighting
- gentle environmental movement

Animations should reinforce immersion rather than spectacle.

---

# Future

Possible future additions include:

- customizable Room layouts
- animated environmental details
- personalized decorations
- additional architectural styles
- larger multi-level Rooms
