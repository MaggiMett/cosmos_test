# Workspace

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Authoritative  
**Category:** Workspace

---

# Purpose

Workspaces are dedicated environments for focused activities inside Cosmos.

Unlike traditional software, Workspaces are represented by physical furniture integrated into the Base and Rooms.

They should always feel like places to work rather than application pages.

---

# Overview

Every Workspace exists in two visual forms.

Closed State

A physical object integrated into the environment.

Open State

An interactive, fixed Workspace Environment Window opened above the environment.

The physical Workspace always remains part of the room.

---

# Object Template

## Physical Representation

Every Workspace is represented by a unique piece of furniture.

Examples include:

- bookshelf
- workbench
- drafting table
- laboratory desk
- archive cabinet

Each Workspace should be immediately recognizable while closed.

---

## Placement

Workspaces are permanently integrated into the surrounding room.

They never appear as floating interface elements.

Their position contributes to the room's architecture.

---

## Interaction

Selecting a Workspace opens its corresponding Workspace Environment Window.

The physical Workspace itself remains visible behind the opened Window.

---

## Recognition

Every Workspace should communicate its purpose through its physical appearance.

Users should recognize a Workspace before interacting with it.

---

# Visual Identity

A Workspace is recognized by:

- unique furniture
- permanent physical placement
- recognizable silhouette
- thematic decoration
- architectural integration

Every Workspace should feel like part of the room.

---

# Cosmos Theme

## Appearance

The Cosmos Theme defines:

- materials
- colors
- lighting
- decorative objects
- ambient details

Each Workspace should visually communicate its purpose.

---

## Decoration

Decoration reinforces the Workspace identity.

Examples include:

Knowledge Workspace

- books
- maps
- plants
- documents

Creation Workspace

- tools
- blueprints
- prototypes
- mechanical parts

Decoration should remain functional rather than excessive.

---

# Visual States

Every Workspace supports:

- default
- highlighted
- selected
- opened

Opening a Workspace never removes the physical furniture.

---

# Animations

Version 1 animations remain restrained.

Examples include:

- subtle highlight on interaction
- smooth Workspace opening
- gentle ambient lighting
- soft transition into the Workspace Environment Window

Animations should reinforce immersion.

---

# Future

Possible future additions include:

- animated furniture
- customizable Workspace furniture
- additional Workspace themes
- interactive decorative objects
- personalized room layouts
