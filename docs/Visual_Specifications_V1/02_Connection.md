# Connection

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Authoritative  
**Category:** Cosmos Object

---

# Purpose

Connections visually communicate relationships between Nodes.

They guide the user's eye through the Cosmos while remaining secondary to the Nodes themselves.

Connections should never dominate the visual composition.

---

# Overview

Connections link related Nodes together.

They form the visible constellations inside Projects and help users understand structure at a glance.

---

# Object Template

## Layout

Every Connection links exactly two Nodes.

The interactive hitbox follows a straight line between both Nodes.

The visible appearance may differ from the hitbox.

---

## Shape

The visible Connection is a soft, wave-like energy beam.

The wave should remain subtle and natural.

It should never resemble lightning or a rigid zigzag.

---

## Thickness

Connection thickness communicates visual importance.

More important relationships appear slightly thicker.

The difference should remain subtle.

---

## Direction

Connections have no visible direction.

Version 1 intentionally avoids arrows or directional indicators.

---

## Crossing

Connections may cross naturally.

Crossings receive no special visual treatment.

---

# Visual Identity

A Connection is recognized by:

- a flowing energy-like appearance
- smooth wave motion
- subtle glow
- visual support rather than dominance

---

# Cosmos Theme

## Appearance

Connections resemble soft streams of energy connecting stars.

They visually belong to the surrounding Project.

---

## Color

Connections inherit a blended appearance from the connected Nodes.

The transition should feel smooth and harmonious.

---

## Glow

A subtle glow improves readability against the dark Cosmos background.

The glow should remain weaker than the glow of Nodes.

---

# Visual States

Connections support:

- default
- active
- highlighted
- search result

Hovering a Connection produces no additional visual effect.

---

# Animations

Version 1 animations remain restrained.

Examples include:

- gentle energy flow
- subtle wave movement
- smooth appearance
- soft fade transitions

Animations should reinforce the feeling of living energy.

---

# Future

Possible future additions include:

- animated particle flow
- theme-specific connection styles
- constellation effects
- dynamic energy intensity
